About PlayHub

PlayHub is a small, free directory of free browser games. It is not a proxy: it never mirrors, scrapes or re-hosts anyone else's games, code or images.

How things appear here

  • Every card opens the game on its original site in a new tab, so the game runs on the site that owns it.
  • A game is embedded here only if its platform or developer clearly allows it.
  • Every card names its source and links back to it.

Credits

All titles and artwork belong to their creators and the platforms that host them: Poki, CrazyGames, GamePix, itch.io and WatchDocumentaries. A listing is not an endorsement, and PlayHub is not affiliated with any of them.

Privacy

PlayHub sets no cookies and runs no analytics. Favorites and recently played stay in your own browser. The sites you open from PlayHub are third-party sites with their own cookies and privacy rules.

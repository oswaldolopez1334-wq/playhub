About PlayHub

PlayHub is a small, free directory of HTML5 games and full-length documentaries. It is not a proxy: it never mirrors, scrapes or re-hosts anyone else's games, code, images or videos.

How things appear here

  • Games are embedded only when the platform or developer allows it, such as itch.io's embed option, a publisher program or an open license. Otherwise the card opens the original page in a new tab.
  • Documentaries are embedded only from YouTube videos that allow embedding. Otherwise we link out.
  • Every card names its source and links back to it.

Credits

All titles, artwork and videos belong to their creators and the platforms that host them: Poki, CrazyGames, GamePix, itch.io, WatchDocumentaries and YouTube. A listing is not an endorsement, and PlayHub is not affiliated with any of them.

Privacy

PlayHub sets no cookies and runs no analytics. Favorites and recently played stay in your own browser. Games and videos are third-party content and may set their own cookies once you press Play.
